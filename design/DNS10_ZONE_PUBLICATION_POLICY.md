# DNS10 — snapshot ordering and resource policy

Status: pure source-only policy, not an authenticated receiver or storage implementation. PR67 `cb83531ac5a122b978378e2568a5407b7e551eba` remains the governing product design. Nothing here creates, updates or deletes production data.

The future receiver authenticates with a distinct source-scoped upload credential, validates the complete request and obtains the dataset, binding, sequence state, accounted payload totals and free-space evidence from trusted server-side sources. All authorization, version checks, state reads, policy application and publication must be serialized in one database transaction. It computes SHA-256 and byte length from the exact received raw body itself; neither value is trusted from the collector.

Per dataset, only a sequence newer than the last accepted one can publish. An equal sequence is an idempotent acknowledgment only when the exact-payload digest and length also match; it must not modify any record or refresh any collection, receive or publication time. An older sequence, or an equal sequence with a different digest or byte count, fails closed. Sequence is bounded to 1..2^53−1. Missing or lost collector sequence state is not a reason to reset the server counter.

The accepted raw body is 1..4 MiB inclusive. Logical generation payload accounting is capped at 256 MiB across the integration. The caller supplies the authoritative current total and the exact payload size of the previous generation that will be removed atomically after a new generation is inserted; the resulting current-plus-previous generation total must fit the budget. Before inserting, conservatively require filesystem free space minus the whole new payload to remain at least 512 MiB. This accounts for the temporary overlap; it is not a guarantee about database index, redo or physical tablespace growth, which must be measured independently before rollout. Validate inconsistent or untrusted counters as errors. No cleanup of unrelated files or database records is authorized.

This policy returns `publish` with the proposed accounted total, or `replay` with the existing total. It does not authorize an upload, generate a digest, inspect a filesystem, reserve space, persist a sequence, delete a generation, or publish a snapshot. A future storage adapter must ensure those guarantees under real concurrent transactions and preserve the last good snapshot on any failure.
